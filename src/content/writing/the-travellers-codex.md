---
title: "The Traveller's Codex"
description: "Life is a journey..."
type: "poem"
---

The road must not be precise - take any,  
But the path should lead up the valley&#39;s rise,  
The crest however isn&#39;t not unworthy,  
It is climbing that gives it a price.

Let the journey take you wholly,  
Drink its thorns, eat with morning dew,  
Dance Davidly with every forward step,  
Linger not on the abandoned roads.

At midday you will come upon a ragged herder,  
He offers his rod; prepared to submit,  
You&#39;re alike, remember not your father&#39;s,  
Kneel before him if you are to arrive.
